# Spec 17.6 — Records on the workout summary: one ledger, first times kept quiet, a calmer page order (web)

**Status:** v0.1 (2026-11-08). **Phase A built** (2026-11-10, branch `feat/spec-17-6a-record-ledger`); Phase B not started. The owner approved the ledger design, Phase B and the collapse numbers on 2026-11-08.

**Source:** the whole-app Impeccable critique of 2026-11-08 (23/40), step 3 of its agreed fix order. The owner's complaint: "the way PRs are displayed after the workout is finished looks awkward and is hard to understand." The owner decided on 2026-11-08 that a first-ever number is a quiet line, not brass and not counted (D1). A decision marked "accepted recommendation" came from the design review or the drafting, and the owner accepted it on 2026-11-08.

**Design refs:** Spec 17.2 Phase B (record cards, rounding, D13, D16, D17) and AC8; Spec 08.0 (the summary, `section`); Spec 07.0 (`records.ts`, D16, D18 "held now"); Spec 07.3 (`recordCount`); Spec 18.0 AC1; Spec 20.2 AC1 and D13; #107 (the Workout menu); `apps/web/DESIGN.md` (Earned Brass, Single Meaning, Tint Not Outline); `apps/web/PRODUCT.md` (no streaks, badges or gamification).

## 1. Purpose, scope & non-goals

**Purpose.** The summary is the one screen where the app says "you got stronger", and it says it badly. On a phone the records start at the bottom edge, below the routine note, notes, stats, Edit workout and a Hevy prompt. Then "6 new" sits above 3 cards. Each card has an unlabeled "82.5 kg × 5" in the corner, repeats 82.5 in the figure, and spreads "+2.5 kg", "was 80 kg" and a bare "Est. 1RM" chip over four lines. A first-ever lift counts and gets brass though it beat nothing.

**In scope.**
- **Phase A (PR 1): the records.** One brass panel that reads like a ledger; first times as quiet lines; one count that means one thing; the "Est. 1-rep max" term explained; a collapse for long lists; records placed right after the date; wording that is true on a workout from months ago.
- **Phase B (PR 2): the page order.** The routine note, Edit workout and the Hevy block move out of the way of the numbers.

**Non-goals.**
- Any API or `@sin/core` change (§12 lists three deferred follow-ups).
- Changing how records are computed, stored or rebuilt (Spec 07.0). All record math stays in `@sin/core`; this spec formats what the API serves.
- Motion, confetti, sound, share cards, streaks, badges, "you beat last week" copy (Spec 17.2 and PRODUCT.md).
- Set-volume records: still not shown on the summary (17.2 AC16).
- Progress's chart, the exercise page and Progress's records list (only their label for est. 1RM, D6).
- The History row's trophy and its "N PRs" (§12, F1).

## 2. Acceptance criteria

Terms. A **lift** is one exercise lineage (`exerciseId`). A **beaten** record has `previousValue !== null`; a **first** record has `previousValue === null`. Set-volume records are filtered out first (17.2 AC16), as today.

### Phase A — the ledger

1. **Where it sits.** The records come right after the title, date and "From routine" lines, above the routine note, the workout notes and the stats row. The summary never waits on the records query (08.0 AC19, 17.2 AC28): while it is pending nothing renders, and after a failure the existing notice shows beside what is already there.
2. **One panel.** All lifts with at least one beaten record render in **one** panel on `--color-pr-soft` (Tint Not Outline: no outline). The panel is a `section` named by its `h2`. Each lift is an `li` in a `ul` with its name as `h3`, over its rows. No per-lift card.
3. **The heading counts lifts.** The `h2` reads "New records on 3 lifts" (for one lift with more than one row, "New records on 1 lift"; for one row in all, "New record"). It is brass. There is no separate "N new" count, and the number is never a count of rows. A first record never adds to it.
4. **A row is a ledger line.** Each row reads: a muted label, then the old value, an arrow and the new value with one shared unit, then the gain in brass. Example: `Heaviest weight   80 → 82.5 kg   +2.5 kg`. The new value is ink and bold, the old value and arrow are muted. When a row does not fit on one line at 390 px (long labels, seconds in words), the label takes its own line above the figure; nothing clips and nothing scrolls sideways at 320 px.
5. **Units and gains.** Values follow the profile unit (`unitPreference`). The old value drops the unit when the new one carries it and the unit is a suffix (kg, lb, reps, cal, m); a duration reads in words on both sides ("1 min 30 s → 1 min 45 s"). Gains are as in 17.2 AC22: the exact difference through `formatRecordValue`, except est. 1-rep max, whose old and new values are rounded first (`roundedE1rm`) and whose gain is the difference of the rounded values in integer steps. A new helper in `features/units/format.ts` builds the pair. No arithmetic on record values happens anywhere else in the web, and none leaves `@sin/core`.
6. **A zero-gain estimate is dropped (17.2 D16, kept).** If an est. 1-rep max's rounded gain is not a rise, its row is not shown while the lift has another beaten row. If it is the lift's only beaten row, it shows as `Est. 1-rep max   94 kg` with no old value and no gain. The lift still counts.
7. **Row types.** Rows come in `RECORD_TYPE_VALUES` order, set volume excluded. The labels are: Heaviest weight, Est. 1-rep max, Most reps, Most calories, Longest distance, Longest hold. A record distance is meters for every account (18.1 D6). The type-by-modality table is in §5.
8. **Where the set came from.** When every row of a lift has the same `sourceSetId` and that set is in the workout, one muted line follows its rows: "from your 82.5 kg × 5" (`formatSet`, as logged). When the sets differ, each row carries its own "from your …" line under its figure. A set that is not in the workout is omitted. The corner text of the old card is gone.
9. **The term is explained.** When any est. 1-rep max row is rendered, the panel ends with one muted line: "Est. 1-rep max: what you could likely lift once, worked out from weight × reps." It appears once per panel and never when no such row shows.
10. **Order and wrap.** Lifts follow the workout's exercise order, then name (17.2 AC25, D17 kept). Names wrap (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`).
11. **Long lists collapse.** With more than 4 lifts the panel shows the first 3 and a **Show all 7 lifts** button (a `secondary` or quieter `Button`, 44 px, `aria-expanded`); expanded, it reads **Show fewer**. The heading's number counts every lift whatever is shown. The state is local and not stored. With 4 lifts or fewer there is no button. The first-time list (AC13) uses the same rule on its own.

### Phase A — first times

12. **A first-ever number is a quiet line (owner, D1).** A lift whose beaten rows are none and whose records are all first gets one line, outside the panel: "First time logged: Triceps Pushdown 32.5 kg". It is muted text, no brass, no tint, no icon, and it is not counted in AC3. The value is the lift's lead record by `RECORD_TYPE_VALUES` order (weight, else reps, calories, distance or time); if the lead is an est. 1-rep max the line says "…, est. 1-rep max 96.5 kg". The line is a list item in a `ul` named "First time logged".
13. **Mixed lifts and layout.** A lift with a beaten row and a first row shows only the beaten rows in the panel (an estimate that is new only because earlier sets had more than 12 reps is not news). The quiet lines come after the panel, in the workout's order. With more than 4 of them, 3 show with "Show all 6 first times" (AC11's rule). With no beaten record there is no panel and no brass anywhere on the screen.
14. **No set-volume or timed-result rows.** Set volume is not shown (17.2 AC16). A timed section's score and round times are not records (07.0 has no type for them) and are never listed here.

### Phase A — wording, reuse, a11y

15. **The same screen in History.** `section="workouts"` (just finished) reads "New records…"; `section="history"` reads "Records from this workout" followed by the same lift count ("Records from this workout on 3 lifts"), because a workout from months ago did not just set them. The rows, the quiet lines and the explanation are identical. (Records are what the workout *holds now*, 07.0 D18 and 17.2 AC34: beating one later removes it here. No note is added.)
16. **One vocabulary for est. 1RM (D6).** *Widened (Carol, 2026-11-08):* the chart's metric label (`METRICS.est1rm.label`: the chip, the caption and the chart's accessible summary) reads "Est. 1-rep max" too. `recordLabel("best_est_1rm")` reads "Est. 1-rep max" everywhere it is used, so Progress's records list and the exercise page match the ledger. `shortRecordLabel` and the chips are removed with `recordCards.ts`, `RecordCardList.tsx` and their tests (replaced by the new model and component).
17. **Accessible.** Each row's accessible text is one sentence ("Heaviest weight: was 80 kg, now 82.5 kg, up 2.5 kg"); the arrow and the visible gain are `aria-hidden`. Heading order is `h1` > `h2` > `h3`. Nothing relies on color. Nothing is announced on arrival and there is no live region (17.2 AC13). Axe-clean in light and dark.
18. **Brass is earned.** Brass appears only in the panel's heading, its gains and its tint (`--color-pr`, `--color-pr-soft`). The panel's CSS module replaces `RecordCardList.module.css` in `PR_ALLOWED` (`roles.test.ts`). The quiet lines and the collapse button live in files that are not on the list. No hard-coded color or px; any new size is a named token (`--record-label-width` for the label column).
19. **No motion.** The new CSS has no `animation`, `@keyframes` or `transition` (17.2 AC13's scan is extended to it).
20. **Fixtures.** The screenshot harness covers: screenshot 11's workout (three lifts, an est. 1-rep max on each; heading "New records on 3 lifts"); 27 (a first-ever lift only: one quiet line, no panel); 28 and 29 (none, and volume only: no section); 65 (calories and weighted carry rows, with seconds and meters); 97 (timed sections: no records); a workout with 7 lifts (collapsed and expanded); a mixed workout (a panel plus quiet lines); and `section="history"`, light and dark, 390 and 1280 px, and 320 × 568. Fixture numbers change ("6 new" and "2 new" are gone).

### Phase B — page order

21. **The order.** Back and Workout menu; the `h1`; title, date, "From routine"; records (Phase A); stats; workout notes; exercises and sections; the Hevy block (owner only); the routine note. The `h1` text "Workout summary" does not change (the e2e smoke asserts it).
22. **Edit workout moves into the Workout menu** (beside Delete workout, which stays danger and last). It navigates exactly as today, with no request of its own. It is no longer on the page under the stats.
23. **The routine note moves to the bottom** as plain text under a muted label, "Routine note", only when `routineNotes` is set. It is not hidden in a disclosure.
24. **The Hevy block moves** after the exercises with its content and behavior unchanged (20.2's own states are not touched).

### Docs and verification

25. **Docs consistent.** The cross-doc updates in §12 are made.
26. **Every behavioral AC has a tagged test.** AC1–AC19 and AC21–AC24 have ≥1 test named `17.6 ACn — …`, enforced by an `acTags.17-6.test.ts` scan.
27. **Phone check (owner).** On the iPhone, with a real just-finished workout and with an old one opened from History: the first screen after the title shows the records; the numbers read without explanation; a new lift reads as a quiet line.

## 6. Behavior & logic

### 6.1 `buildRecordLedger`

A pure function replacing `buildRecordCards` and `recordsNewCount`. The file is `recordLedger.ts`; the component is `RecordLedgerPanel.tsx` (a `RecordLedger.tsx` would collide with it on case-insensitive disks).

```ts
visible = records without best_set_volume
byLift  = group visible by exerciseId
for each lift:
  beaten = rows with previousValue !== null, in RECORD_TYPE_VALUES order
  drop an est. 1RM row whose rounded gain is not a rise, unless it is the only beaten row (then: value only)
  if beaten is empty -> firsts.push(line from the lift's lead record)
  else -> lifts.push({ name, rows, source: shared sourceSetId or per-row })
lifts: workout order, then name
heading: from lifts.length, rows count and `section`
```

### 6.2 Why a count of lifts

"New records on 3 lifts" is a count a lifter can check against the page. A count of record rows reads as "6" over three names, because one heavy set often beats both weight and estimate. A first record is not a record that beat anything, so it joins neither the heading nor the brass.

### 6.3 First-principles note: why "held now" matters here

The API stores a record only while it is the best (07.0 D18). A summary read months later lists what that workout still holds, and the `previousValue` it shows is the best before that workout. So the numbers stay true, but the word "New" would not: hence AC15.

## 12. Decisions & open questions

- ✅ **D1 — A first-ever number is a quiet line (owner, 2026-11-08).** "First time logged: Triceps Pushdown 32.5 kg": no brass, not counted. Reverses Spec 17.2 D13 and AC19 and the counting in AC26. **Rationale:** brass is for beating something. **Consequence:** History's server count still counts them (F1).
- ✅ **D2 — One panel with one row per record, headed by a count of lifts (owner, 2026-11-08).** The ledger reads left to right (label, old → new, gain) and puts the set below as one line. **Rejected:** a card per lift (the awkward look of today); a one-line "N new" over rows (it counts rows).
- ✅ **D3 — Records sit right after the date, before stats (owner, 2026-11-08).** It answers "starts at the bottom edge", and stats are a second thing to read. The stats row follows. **Rejected:** keeping the stats first.
- ✅ **D4 — Long lists collapse after 4 lifts, to 3 (owner, 2026-11-08, accepted recommendation).** A new-program day can have many first times and would push the exercises off the screen.
- ✅ **D5 — History says "Records from this workout" (owner, 2026-11-08).** Chosen by the `section` prop, not by the clock, so it is deterministic and testable. **Rejected:** a time cut-off ("New" for 12 hours).
- ✅ **D6 — "Est. 1-rep max" replaces "Best est. 1RM" and "Est. 1RM", the chart's metric label included (owner, 2026-11-08).** "1RM" is gym slang that new users may not know, and the two spellings already differ between the card chip and Progress. The explanation line lives on the summary only. **Deferred:** the same sentence on the exercise page and the chart (the Exercises tab owner, Spec 18.2).
- ✅ **D7 — Edit workout goes into the Workout menu; the routine note goes to the bottom; Hevy goes after the exercises (owner, 2026-11-08, Phase B).** #107's rule applies: rare actions lead the page, in the menu. Editing a past workout is a fix, not part of reading it. The Hevy block keeps its states. **Rejected:** Edit and Hevy as one row under the stats (still above the exercises); a disclosure for the routine note (hides text for no gain at the bottom). **Open (O2):** the owner may prefer Edit visible; then Phase B keeps it as a secondary button under the exercises.
- ✅ **D8 — Records stay held-now (07.0 D18, 17.2 AC34).**

### Deferred follow-ups (API; another session works in `apps/api`)

- **F1 — History's `recordCount` counts the wrong thing.** After 07.3 it counts every non-volume record row the workout holds, including first records and counting one lift twice (weight and estimate). Summary and History therefore disagree: "New records on 3 lifts" beside a "6 PRs" trophy. **Meanwhile:** History keeps "N PRs" as served (the web never re-derives it, 17.2 AC32 and 07.3), the summary shows no total that could be compared with it, and 17.2 AC32 (equal numbers) is retired by this spec. **Decided (owner, 2026-11-08):** do it, after Spec 21.1 finishes, in Spec 07.6: count lifts with at least one beaten non-volume record, so the trophy and the heading mean the same thing.
