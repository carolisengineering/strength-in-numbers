# Spec 17.10 — Sheets: one bottom action row, hints said once (web)

**Status:** v0.3 (2026-11-11). Phase A merged (#309); Phase B implemented (branch `feat/spec-17-10b-remaining-sheets`): every sheet uses the row.

**Source:** the whole-app Impeccable critique of 2026-11-08 (23/40). The owner has asked for the specs of the whole fix order before any building. This spec covers the sheets finding; the decisions below need the owner's OK before Phase A starts.

**Design refs:** `apps/web/DESIGN.md` (Thumb Rule, The ActionBar, "A full sheet is the Stone Ground itself"); Spec 06.0 AC24 (`Sheet` is a native modal `<dialog>`); Spec 06.1 AC32; DM-12 (`dismissible`); `apps/web/CLAUDE.md` "iPhone keyboard frame" (#204, #206, #207); Spec 10.7 AC1–AC3 (the clock field); Spec 17.4 D4 and D5 (the workout bar, its status line). Neighbors: Specs 17.5 (Undo, Delete set), 17.7 (`ui/Segmented`, `ui/Select`), 17.8 (entry panel), 17.9 (docked panel), 17.11 (the routine item sheet's rows), 17.13 (copy and small fixes).

## 1. Purpose, scope & non-goals

**Purpose.** A sheet is full height, but its content starts at the top and its buttons come right after the last field, so on a 390 × 844 phone Save sits at about y = 265 and Close is at the very top: both out of reach of a thumb. The same app's `ConfirmDialog` already hugs the bottom edge for this reason. Five different action layouts also exist across the sheets (the review counted four). This spec gives every sheet one action row at the bottom of the visible rectangle.

**What was checked (verified in code and screenshots).**
- `ui/Sheet.tsx` renders `header` (Close, title) then one scrolling `.body`; nothing is anchored to the bottom, and nothing reads `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.
- Layouts today: Cancel left and Save right with `space-between` (ResultSheet, workouts `SectionSheet`, `SetEditSheet` with Delete set left); both buttons grouped at the right (`CreateExerciseForm`, `EditExerciseSheet`); a block button (routine `ItemSheet`, routine `SectionSheet`, `ChangeTimeSheet`, `AddSectionSheet`); natural width on the left (the "Add set" button); and `NoteSheet`, which puts Save before Cancel. That is five, not four.
- The hint "Type the digits: 341 is 3:41." comes from `ClockField`, which appends it to every field. `ResultSheet` draws one `ClockField` per round, so a five-round Set Timer shows it five times (screenshot 93).

**In scope.**
1. A bottom-anchored action row owned by `Sheet`, used by every sheet.
2. The keyboard frame, long sheets, focus order and focus-not-obscured for that row.
3. The clock hint said once per group.
4. Which sheets migrate, and in how many PRs.

**Non-goals.**
- `ConfirmDialog` (already bottom-hugging; its buttons are natural width and right-aligned, which is a smaller inconsistency, listed as a follow-up in §12).
- What a sheet contains: Spec 17.11 (item sheet rows), 17.7 (dropdowns), 17.13 (radio style, the "Modality" label).
- Desktop presentation of a sheet (a side drawer instead of a 40rem column): Spec 17.12 §12.
- Undo and Delete-set protection: Spec 17.5. The Delete set button only changes place here.
- Any API, schema or `@sin/core` change. **No API change is needed.**

## 2. Acceptance criteria

### The row

1. **`Sheet` owns the row; sheets fill it.** `Sheet` renders, after the scrolling body and as a sibling of it, an element with `data-sheet-actions` holding three slots in this order: danger, dismiss, primary. `Sheet` draws the dismiss button itself (`dismissLabel`, default "Cancel"). A new `SheetActions` component, rendered anywhere inside a sheet's children, puts its `danger` and `primary` buttons into their slots (a React portal, so state and handlers stay where they are) and its `status` above the row. A sheet that renders no `SheetActions` still gets the dismiss button. `Sheet`'s props, final: `open`, `title`, `onClose`, `dismissible`, `dismissLabel`, `onDismiss`, `children`.
2. **One layout.** In the row, each button takes an equal share of the width, is at least `--tap-target-min` tall, and the primary one is last. The row wraps rather than clipping when three labels don't fit; there are never more than three buttons. A sheet has at most one `primary` `Button`.
3. **Dismiss is the same as Close.** The dismiss button calls `onClose`. While `dismissible` is false (a save in flight, DM-12) it is `aria-disabled` and does nothing, exactly as the header's Close is, and keeps focus. No sheet renders its own Cancel any more.
4. **Status line.** `SheetActions`' `status`, when given, shows as one muted `--font-size-sm` line centered above the buttons (as `ActionBarHint` does) and is not a live region unless the caller makes it one.
5. **Anchored to the bottom.** With short content the row is still at the bottom of the dialog, not under the last field. With long content the row stays put and only the body scrolls; the dialog itself never scrolls.
6. **Submit.** A sheet keeps its own `<form id>`; its primary is `type="submit"` with `form={id}`, so it submits that form from the row. Enter in a single-line field submits through it, and does nothing while that button is disabled. A textarea's Enter still adds a line.
7. **No button in the body.** Every migrated sheet's primary, dismiss and danger buttons are inside `[data-sheet-actions]`. Buttons that act on the content ("Add set", "Plan each set", "Clear result", starters) stay in the body.

### The keyboard, scrolling and focus

8. **On the keyboard.** While `data-keyboard="open"` is set on `<html>`, the dialog is the visible rectangle (unchanged, #206), so the row sits on the keyboard's top edge, and its bottom padding drops the home-indicator inset.
9. **Home indicator.** With the keyboard down, the row's bottom padding adds `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, so a button is never under the indicator.
10. **Focus order.** Tab order is Close, the body's fields, then danger, dismiss, primary. `showModal()`'s first focus still lands on Close (no text field, so no keyboard raised).
11. **Focus not obscured (WCAG 2.4.11).** The row is a sibling of the scroller, not an overlay on it, so a focused or revealed body control is never behind it. The body adds `scroll-padding-block` of `--space-4` so a field focused by Tab is not flush with an edge.
12. **Long sheets.** A sheet taller than the visible rectangle (the item sheet with five sets, the result sheet with five rounds, a sheet with the keyboard up) scrolls inside `.body`; the header and the row stay visible.

### Hints

13. **The clock hint is said once per group.** `ClockField` takes an optional `describedBy` and `hint={null}` mode. In `ResultSheet`, the rounds group shows "Type the digits: 341 is 3:41." once, in the group's existing hint, and each round's field points at it with `aria-describedby`. A lone `ClockField` (a section's time limit, For Time's finish) still shows its own hint, so screenshots 90 and 95 are unchanged.

### Migration

14. **Phase A sheets** use the row: `SetEditSheet` (danger Delete set; primary Save, or Add set for a new set), routine `ItemSheet` (primary Done), routine `SectionSheet` (primary Done), `ResultSheet` (primary Save), `EditExerciseSheet` and `CreateExerciseForm` in both sheets that host it (primary Save), `NoteSheet`, `ChangeTimeSheet`, `DetailsSheet`.
15. **Phase B sheets** use it: workouts `SectionSheet` (danger Remove section, primary Save), `AddSectionSheet` (primary Add; its title reads Add section), `MoveToSectionSheet` (dismiss only), `ExercisePicker`'s list view (dismiss only; its create view is AC14's), `LoadMappingSheet`, `PlaceholderSheet`, `MatchForHevySheet`.
16. **Labels are unchanged.** Every button keeps its present text and accessible name, so a test that finds "Save" or "Cancel" by role still finds exactly one.

### Must keep

17. **Regression guards.** The existing `Sheet.test.tsx` suites pass with no edits: AC24 (labelled modal, Close first in DOM order, Escape calls `onClose`), AC32 (a native close reaches the parent), DM-12, and #206's CSS rule text. Existing sheet tests pass apart from the changes §10 lists.
18. **No new color or value.** The row uses existing tokens only: `--space-*`, `--tap-target-min`, `--color-bg`, `--color-line`, `--color-muted`, `--font-size-sm`. `assert:css-tokens` and `roles.test.ts` pass untouched.

### Docs and verification

19. **Real-browser check.** The screenshot harness captures each migrated sheet (light and dark, 390 × 844) and fails the run if the row's bottom edge is not at the viewport bottom (allowing the safe-area inset), if any control in the body is covered by the row, or if a row button is under `--tap-target-min`. It captures one sheet with the keypad up (as `01k` does for the session) and checks the row rests on the frame's bottom edge, and one at 320 × 568 with three buttons.
20. **Docs consistent.** The cross-doc updates in §12 are made.
21. **Every behavioral AC has a tagged test.** AC1–AC18 have ≥1 test named `17.10 ACn — …`, enforced by an `acTags.17-10.test.ts` scan.
22. **Phone check (owner).** On the iPhone: open the set edit sheet, the note sheet and Change Time; confirm Save is under the thumb without reaching, the keypad pushes the row up with it, nothing sits under the home indicator, and Cancel does what Close does.

## 3. Dependencies & exposed interface

**Needs:** nothing from 17.4–17.9. It composes with them:
- **17.5** replaces the Delete / Save row with an inline confirm ("Delete set 3? 47.5 kg × 6 will be removed.", Keep set, Delete set). That maps onto this row directly: the question is `SheetActions`' `status`, and the buttons swap in the slots. Whichever lands second adapts; neither blocks the other.
- **17.7** replaces the `<select>`s inside sheets with `Segmented` and `Select`. Independent; the row has no select.
- **17.11** rewrites the item sheet's body. Both touch `ItemSheet.tsx`; 17.10 lands first, so 17.11 edits the body only.
- **17.8** Phase B changes `SetEditSheet`'s steppers and leaves its action row alone (its own text says so), which is this spec's part; the two edit different parts of the file.
- **17.12** (desktop) may give the sheet a different outer shell; it keeps this row.
- **17.13** uses `status` for the create-exercise "why Save is off" line.

**Exposes:** `Sheet`'s props, final: `open`, `title`, `onClose`, `dismissible`, `dismissLabel`, `onDismiss`, `children` (Phase A's temporary `noActionRow` is gone); the `SheetActions` component (`primary`, `danger`, `status`, `notice`), and `data-sheet-actions` / `data-sheet-body` for tests and the harness.

**What the code required (Phase A, 2026-11-11).**
- **AC3 + `onDismiss`.** The picker's create view must keep its Cancel going back to the list with the search kept (06.0 AC28's pinned test), while Close leaves the picker. So `Sheet` takes `onDismiss` for the row's button only (default `onClose`); Close and Escape always call `onClose`.
- **AC4 + `notice`.** With Save pinned to the bottom, a failure at the end of a long body can be out of view. `SheetActions` takes a `notice` (the caller's own `role="alert"` or `InlineNotice`), shown above `status`. Every migrated form sheet's save failure goes there; field errors stay under their fields.
- **§6.3, no `inSheet`.** Both hosts of `CreateExerciseForm` are sheets, so the prop's other branch would be dead. The form always renders Save through `SheetActions` and loses `onCancel`; Cancel is the host sheet's.
- **§6.4, create hosts stay dismissible.** Neither create host latches `dismissible`, because closing mid-save is allowed (the form's `mounted` guard drops the late answer). So their Cancel follows Close and is no longer disabled during a save; `CreateExerciseForm.test.tsx`'s "disables Cancel while a save is in flight" is replaced by "Cancel during a save ends the form". `EditExerciseSheet` already latches, so its Cancel is `aria-disabled` while saving.
- **AC13, Set Timer.** A Set Timer's rounds sit directly in the form, with no group and no hint, so it gets one new hint line above its rounds (`result-clock-hint`). AMRAP and Time Cap's round-ends group's hint becomes "Type the gym clock when each round ended. Type the digits: 341 is 3:41." with the same id. Only one renders.
- **17.5's confirm.** `SetDeleteConfirm` is gone: its question and two buttons now go to three slots (`SetSheetActions`). Keep set takes Delete set's place (so a double tap keeps the set) and the confirm's Delete set takes Save's, with the question as the status line and Keep set's description. `useDeleteConfirm` focuses Keep set (`keepRef`). The confirm is no longer a `role="group"`.

**What the code required (Phase B, 2026-11-11).**
- **AC15, Add section.** The routine `AddSectionSheet`'s button reads "Add" (the title reads Add section), and AC16 keeps labels, so its primary is Add.
- **AC15, the load sheet.** While Hevy's list is read or the file is checked, the row's Cancel does what the in-body Cancel did: it stops the paced reads and goes back to Choose a file (`onDismiss`), without closing. Close still leaves. In the preview, Cancel closes; the Load button is the primary, still described by the leave-out note. A failed replace is the row's `notice` (the plan had kept it in the alert region at the top of the body, which a long preview scrolls out of view; final review); the choose step's errors stay in that region, mounted empty from first paint (minor 185).
- **AC15, the markers sheet.** Save markers shows only once Hevy's list has loaded; with no key, while loading and after a failed read, the row is Cancel alone. A failed save is the row's `notice`.
- **AC15, Match for Hevy.** Continue keeps its secondary look (20.2's) in the primary slot. "At least one exercise has to go to Hevy." is the row's `status` (it says why Continue is off); a failed save is the `notice`. Each card's own Cancel (pick and create modes) stays in the body: it acts on the card, not the sheet (AC7).
- **AC15, the workout section sheet.** "Its exercises and sets stay in the workout, without a section." stays in the body, last, under its divider, and describes the row's Remove section; as the `status` it would sit above Save on every edit.
- **AC15 scan.** `Sheet.actions.test.tsx` fails if any source file names `noActionRow`, or if a file that renders a `Sheet` uses a Cancel copy constant other than as its `dismissLabel` (Match for Hevy's two in-card Cancels are the listed exception). The harness fails an open sheet with no row.

## 6. Behavior & logic

### 6.1 Structure

```
dialog.sheet
  header            Close, title                  (unchanged, first in DOM)
  div .panel        flex: 1; min-height: 0; flex column
    .body           flex: 1; overflow-y: auto; scroll-padding-block   (the sheet's children, incl. its own <form>)
    [data-sheet-actions]   flex: none; padding; background: --color-bg; border-top: 1px solid --color-line
                           slots: danger | dismiss (Sheet's) | primary
```

The row is a flex child below the scroller, so there is no sticky positioning and no overlay: 2.4.11 holds by construction. This differs from `ActionBar` on purpose: `ActionBar` is sticky inside a scroller shared with the page, so it needs `--action-bar-clearance`; a sheet's own scroller ends where the row begins.

### 6.2 The keyboard frame

The sheet's #206 rule gives the dialog `--app-top` and `--app-height`; the row is the last flex child, so it rides on the keyboard with no new code. One addition: `:global(:root[data-keyboard="open"]) .actions { padding-bottom: var(--space-3) }`, since the keyboard covers the home indicator. `trackKeyboardFrame` still scrolls the focused field into view with `block: "nearest"`; inside `.body` it stops at the body's edge, above the row.

### 6.3 `SheetActions`, forms and the portal

Why a portal and not props on `Sheet`: `CreateExerciseForm` is a self-contained form component that owns its `canSave` and `busy` state and draws its own Cancel and Save; `LoadMappingSheet` and `MatchForHevySheet` change their buttons per step. Props on `Sheet` would need that state lifted into every caller. `SheetActions` is rendered where the state is, and the portal puts its buttons in the row.

`Sheet` holds its three slot elements in state through callback refs; `SheetActions` renders `null` until they exist, then portals into them. The state update happens in the commit, before paint, so the row never flashes empty. The buttons are outside the `<form>` in the DOM, so the primary carries `form={id}`; the browser still treats it as that form's default button (the first submit button, in tree order, whose form owner is the form), so Enter works. `CreateExerciseForm` gains `formId` and an `inSheet` prop; with `inSheet` it renders its Cancel and Save through `SheetActions` instead of drawing them in the form (both hosts, the picker's create view and the All exercises sheet, pass it). Risk to check in a spike before Phase A: that jsdom resolves a button's form owner from the `form` attribute, so `userEvent.click` on the row's Save submits the form in tests. If it doesn't, tests dispatch `submit` on the form, and AC6's Enter case is covered by the harness.

### 6.4 Dismiss while busy

`aria-disabled` plus a no-op handler (not `disabled`), so focus isn't thrown to the dialog while a save fails and shows its error: the existing Close behavior (DM-12), extended to the new button. This replaces today's `disabled={busy}` on the sheets' own Cancel buttons.

### 6.5 The clock hint

`ClockField` today builds `hint` from `CLOCK_HINT` always. It gains `describedBy?: string` and treats `hint === null` as "no hint of my own". `ResultSheet` writes the group hint once as `${RESULT_COPY.roundEndsHint} ${CLOCK_HINT}`, gives it an id, and passes it to each round. Copy stays in `timerCopy.ts` and `resultCopy.ts`.

## 12. Decisions & open questions

- ✅ **D1 — One bottom row owned by `Sheet`; sheets fill its slots with `SheetActions` (owner, 2026-11-08, accepted recommendation).** **Rationale:** the finding is that thumb-zone actions are missing and layouts disagree; the only way to make them agree is to take the layout away from each sheet. Buttons stay real elements owned by the sheet, so focus refs and `aria-*` stay on them (workouts `SectionSheet` keeps its `saveRef` and `removeRef`). **Rejected:** props on `Sheet` (they would need `CreateExerciseForm`'s and the Hevy sheets' per-step state lifted into every caller); a sticky bar inside the body as `ActionBar` does (it needs a clearance token per sheet and can still cover a focused field).
- ✅ **D2 — Cancel stays, as well as Close (owner, 2026-11-08, accepted recommendation).** Close is at the top, out of reach, which is the finding. **Rejected:** dropping Cancel and relying on the X.
- ✅ **D3 — Order is danger, dismiss, primary, equal widths (owner, 2026-11-08, accepted recommendation).** The primary is always the last, rightmost, as on every `ActionBar` screen. Delete set and Remove section, rare and destructive, sit at the far end from the primary.
- ✅ **D4 — Choice-only sheets get a Cancel row too (owner, 2026-11-08, accepted recommendation).** `MoveToSectionSheet` and the picker's list view. **Cost:** about 68 px less list height on the picker. **Rejected:** leaving them with Close only, which keeps two behaviors.
- ✅ **D5 — Sheet buttons stay 44 px.** The 56 px of Spec 17.4 D4 is for the one control tapped per set.
- ✅ **D6 — The hint is said once per group, in the group's own hint.** A field alone keeps its hint.

**Settled (owner, 2026-11-08, accepted recommendation): each recommendation below was taken.**
1. D1–D4 as proposed. Recommendation: accept all four. D4 is the one most likely to be reversed; if so, AC15 loses two rows and the dismiss-only case.
2. `ConfirmDialog`'s natural-width, right-aligned buttons: align them with this row later? Recommendation: yes, in a small follow-up after Phase B, not here.
