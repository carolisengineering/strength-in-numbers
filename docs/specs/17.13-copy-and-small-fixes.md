## 1. Purpose, scope & non-goals

**Purpose.** A set of small things that each show a lack of care to a close reader: a British date on every device, jargon in a label, a title said twice, links too short to tap, three kinds of radio button. None is large; together they are what a reviewer notices.

## 2. Acceptance criteria

### Phase A — dates and times (D1)

1. **One module.** `ui/dateText.ts` resolves the locale (D1) and holds the formatters. `formatLocalDate`, `formatStartedTime` and `formatStartedAt` keep their names, arguments and test hooks (`timeZone`, `today`) and are re-exported from `features/workouts/format.ts`. `sentDay` and `oneOffDay` call the same code. A source scan fails if `en-GB` appears anywhere in `src`, if `hourCycle` or `hour12` appears in `ui/dateText.ts`, or if a lifter-visible date is built from an `Intl.DateTimeFormat` outside `ui/dateText.ts` and the Hevy import's month-year helper.
2. **The forms follow the device's locale.** Dates and times use the locale the browser reports (`new Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().locale`), with one set of options: weekday, month and day for a day; the same plus the year where a year shows; hour and minute for a time; and a start on today's calendar day shows the time alone (18.0 AC34). The locale decides the order, the punctuation and 12-hour or 24-hour; the app never forces an hour cycle. Under en-US a day is "Wed, Nov 4"; with the year, "Wed, Nov 4, 2026"; a time, "5:10 PM"; a moment, "Wed, Nov 4, 5:10 PM"; midnight is "12:05 AM" and noon "12:00 PM". Under en-GB the day comes before the month and times are 24-hour.
3. **The fallback is en-US.** When no usable locale is found (the lookup throws, or returns nothing or `und`), the app uses `en-US`. A unit test covers each of those cases by stubbing `Intl`.
4. **Plain spaces.** Modern ICU puts a narrow no-break space (U+202F) before AM and PM. The output replaces it with an ordinary space, so strings are what they look like, copy and paste cleanly, and tests can compare them. A unit test fails on any U+202F.
4a. **Calendar days are stable.** `formatLocalDate("2026-11-04")` is the same day, Wednesday, November 4, in every device zone (it formats the stored date in UTC, as today). Moments follow the device zone; `pinTimeZone` and `inTimeZone` in tests are unchanged.
4b. **Other locales lay out.** The screenshot harness captures the live header, the summary and History under `en-GB` and `de-DE` at 390 px, and a violation of its overlap or pinned-coverage checks fails the run.
5. **Every lifter-visible date goes through it:** the summary and History rows, the edit banner and header, the live header's "Started", the Finish dialogs' "Ends …", Hevy's "Sent to Hevy · Sat, Oct 24", a one-off routine's "until Fri, Oct 30", the Exercises rows' "Last logged …", the Progress readout and sessions list, and the Records list.
6. **Tests keep their exactness.** `src/test/setup.ts` pins the locale to `en-US` through a new `pinLocale` helper (`src/test/locale.ts`, beside `pinTimeZone`), so the suite doesn't depend on the machine running it; the tests of AC2, AC3 and the harness choose their own. The roughly 96 literal date strings in 38 test files and the examples in Specs 10.5, 18.0, 18.2 and 20.2 are updated to the en-US forms. No test is loosened to a pattern.
7. **Nothing else changes.** The web's own download names ("hevy-mapping.csv", "hevy-import-report.csv") contain no date. The account export's name comes from the server's `Content-Disposition` (API, not touched). `localDate`, request bodies and the outbox are unchanged.

### Phase B — copy, titles, reasons

8. **"Type".** `ExerciseFields`' default `modalityLabel` is "Type", the word the Edit sheet and the import card already use. The prop stays.
9. **"Change time".** The live and edit headers' button, the sheet's title, the Finish dialogs' "…as set in Change time." and the Hevy import zone sentence read "Change time". The accessible names follow; the `changeTimeButton` test helper and the tests that name it are updated. "Set Timer", "AMRAP", "Time Cap" and "For Time" are unchanged.
10. **No title said twice.** `Screen` takes `hideTitle`. When set, the `h1` stays in the accessibility tree and is visually hidden. History, Exercises, Profile and Workouts' error view (titled "Workouts") set it, because the app header already says the same word. Workouts' Start view keeps its visible "Start a workout", which doesn't repeat the header (Carol, 2026-11-08). A harness check fails the run on any captured shell screen whose visible `h1` text equals the header's title.
11. **The summary's two titles.** When a workout has its own title, the "Workout summary" `h1` is drawn at `--font-size-lg` and the workout's title stays `--font-size-xl`. With no title of its own the `h1` stays `--font-size-xl`. The `h1`'s text and role don't change.
12. **The page gets about 50 px back.** On History at 390 × 844 the first row starts at least 40 px higher than before (harness measurement).
13. **Why Save is off.** In the create-exercise and Edit sheets, while Save is disabled for an incomplete or unchanged form, one muted line says why: "Enter a name and choose a type." / "Change something to save." It is the sheet row's `status` (Spec 17.10) and Save's `aria-describedby` target; it is gone once Save is enabled. The disabled look is unchanged.
14. **Move says why, everywhere.** A disabled Move up or Move down on the live card and the edit screen's card gives its reason in text in every workout. Headed sections keep "First/Last exercise in this section"; a segment with no heading says "First exercise" and "Last exercise". New constants sit beside `SEGMENT_FIRST_REASON` and `SEGMENT_LAST_REASON`.

### Phase C — targets, radios, the keypad, zoom

15. **Alone-on-a-line links are 44 px.** "Go to Hevy settings" and "Go to the app" use a new `ui/TextLink` (a router `Link` with `display: inline-flex; align-items: center; min-height: var(--tap-target-min)`, underlined as now); the "Show the list" summaries get the same minimum height in their module. A link inside a sentence stays as text and is exempt, which is the rule: a target is 44 px unless it is part of running text.
16. **Radios have two looks.** A new `ui/RadioChip` (the `ExerciseFields` chip: raised surface, `--color-border`, 10 px radius, 44 px tall, the checked one with `--color-selected`) is used by `ExerciseFields`' types, `PlaceholderSheet` and `NameCard`; its `block` variant fills the width for long labels. `ResultSheet`'s Finished / Hit the cap pair uses `Segmented` when Spec 17.7 Phase A has landed, and `RadioChip` if it hasn't. The Theme strip is unchanged. The native radio is drawn at `--radio-size` (1.25rem, a new token) with `accent-color` set to the signal color.
17. **Legends line up.** `reset.css` sets `legend { padding: 0 }`; a legend's text starts at its field's left edge.
18. **The header steps aside for the keypad.** While `data-keyboard="open"` is set on `<html>`, the app header is not displayed and the shell keeps `padding-top: env(safe-area-inset-top)`, so the first row isn't under the status bar. The frame's rule is below 64rem only if Spec 17.12 Phase A has landed (it has no header at wide widths).
19. **Zoom and reflow are measured.** A harness step captures a fixed list of screens (the active workout, the picker, an edit sheet, the item sheet, History, an exercise page, the summary, the routine editor, Profile) at 320 × 568 (reflow, WCAG 1.4.10) and at 390 × 844 with the root font size at 200% (text resize, 1.4.4). It fails on horizontal overflow of the page or of `<main>`, on overlapping controls, and on a primary action that can't be reached (not visible and not scrollable into view). Every finding is fixed in this phase if it is a one-rule fix, otherwise logged in `docs/backlog.md` with a BL number; the PR lists which.
20. **Small targets, made strict.** The harness's `smallTargets` list must be empty on every captured screen, except a native radio or checkbox whose label (or ancestor label) measures at least 44 × 44, which the harness now checks instead of listing. A violation fails the run.
21. **No new color and no hard-coded value.** `--radio-size` is in `tokens.css`; `assert:css-tokens` and `roles.test.ts` pass (`RadioChip`'s selected border uses `--color-selected`, an existing alias).

## 6. Behavior & logic

### 6.1 `ui/dateText.ts`

```ts
const FALLBACK_LOCALE = "en-US";
function deviceLocale(): string {
  try {
    const found = new Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().locale;
    if (found && found !== "und") return found;
  } catch { /* fall through */ }
  return FALLBACK_LOCALE;
}
dayText(date: Date, opts?: { timeZone?: string; year?: boolean }): string   // en-US: "Wed, Nov 4" / "Wed, Nov 4, 2026"
timeText(date: Date, opts?: { timeZone?: string }): string                  // en-US: "5:10 PM", U+202F → " "
```

`formatLocalDate(localDate)` is `dayText(new Date(`${localDate}T00:00:00Z`), { timeZone: "UTC" })`. `formatLocalDateWithYear` (Progress) uses `year: true`; the string concatenation that worked around the British comma is dropped. `formatStartedAt` composes `dayText` and `timeText` and keeps its same-day rule through an `en-CA` day key that is compared, never shown. A moment joins its day and time with `Intl.DateTimeFormat` options together, not a hand-written comma, so the locale decides the separator. `sentDay` and `oneOffDay` become `dayText` calls. One formatter instance per option set is cached, as `format.ts` does now (`Intl` constructors are slow), so a change of device language takes effect on the next page load.

**What "the phone's settings" can reach.** A web page sees only what the browser reports. Whether an iPhone's Region and its 24-Hour Time switch both reach `Intl` has not been observed here, so AC24's phone check records what the phone actually shows. A switch the browser doesn't pass on can't be honored from a web page.

### 6.2 `hideTitle`

`Screen` adds a class that uses `ui/VisuallyHidden`'s technique to the `h1` only. Nothing else about `Screen` changes: the landmark is still labelled by its `h1`.

### 6.3 The keypad header

One rule in `AppShell.module.css`: `:global(:root[data-keyboard="open"]) .header { display: none }`, and the shell's padding-top. Nothing in `trackKeyboardFrame` changes. This can't be verified in the harness (no safe area, no iOS); AC24 is the check. If the phone check shows a problem, the rule is removed and the item moves to Spec 17.9.

### 6.4 The zoom pass

The harness sets `document.documentElement.style.fontSize = "200%"` for the text-resize case (the app's sizes are rem, so they follow it; `--tap-target-min` is a physical px by rule) and uses a 320 × 568 viewport for reflow. 200% browser zoom on a phone-width window is a 195 px layout, narrower than WCAG 1.4.10's 320 px reference; the reflow check at 320 is the one that covers it.

## 12. Decisions & open questions

- ✅ **D1 — Dates and times follow the phone's settings, falling back to en-US (owner, 2026-11-08).** The owner chose this over the recommended fixed en-US. **Consequences:** a lifter or reviewer anywhere sees their own date order and clock. Dates can read differently from one device to another, and in a locale with its own numerals a date's digits may differ from the Latin digits used for weights; that's accepted. Tests stay exact and locale-independent through a pin (AC6), which amends 06.0 AC6's pinned formatters. **Rejected by the owner:** a fixed en-US. **Also rejected:** leaving en-GB; en-US dates with 24-hour times.
- ✅ **D2 — "Change time" in sentence case; timer format names stay as they are (owner, 2026-11-08, accepted recommendation).** "Change Time" is the app's own control label and matches "Edit workout" and "Workout menu". The four format names are names, they are emitted by `@sin/core` into API text and Hevy markers, and the review's own question about them was whether they are names: they are. **Rejected:** lower-casing them (an API-visible change, and "AMRAP" is an acronym).
- ✅ **D3 — The header's title wins; the matching `h1` is hidden, not removed (owner, 2026-11-08, accepted recommendation).** Keeps one `h1` per page for screen readers and for the 183 tests that look for it. **Rejected:** removing the header title (the phone header would hold only a name at one edge); removing the `h1` (an accessibility regression).
- ✅ **D4 — The 44 px rule applies to every target that is not part of running text.** Follows The Thumb Rule; WCAG 2.5.8's 24 px minimum is already met and is not the bar here.
- ✅ **D5 — Hide the app header while the keypad is up (owner, 2026-11-08, accepted recommendation).** It gives back 68 px of a 508 px frame. **Risk:** the status-bar overlap in the Home Screen app, which only a phone shows; AC24 is the check, and the fallback is to defer to 17.9.
- ✅ **D6 — The disabled look of Save stays (Spec 17.0); the reason is added in words (10.2 P6).**

**Settled (owner, 2026-11-08, accepted recommendation): each recommendation below was taken.**
1. D2: leave the timer format names in Title Case (recommended)?
2. D5: try the hidden header, with the phone check as the gate (recommended)?
